    About

    This app draws Bezier curves on a 3D sphere using two geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude). Great for visualizing routes or arcs on a globe.

    How it works

    • You enter two points (lat/lon A and B).
    • Points are converted to 3D positions on the sphere surface.
    • A quadratic Bezier curve is drawn with a control point outside the sphere so the arc “bulges” between the two points.
    • Use OrbitControls: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Pause rotation to inspect the scene.

    Tech

    Built with Three.js, OrbitControls, and jQuery. No build step — open index.html in a browser.
